## 1. SubShield — subcontractor COI tracking
- **Why now:** AI document reading is now cheap and reliable for the standardized ACORD 25 form; insurers keep tightening premium audits; many GCs still run spreadsheets.
- **Why customers pay:** a single uninsured sub at audit costs more than years of subscription.
- **Why they stay:** certificates renew every week of the year, and the audit archive (insurers look back) becomes evidence they can't lose.
- **How I find them:** Google Maps, state contractor license lookups, AGC/ABC/CFMA directories, LinkedIn ("project accountant", "controller").
- **How I contact them:** phone calls to the office (best) + short cold email + broker introductions.
- **What I sell first:** a **free 48-hour COI audit** → a $299 paid 60-day pilot (credited to annual).
- **MVP:** email-in inbox + upload link, AI extraction + human confirm, requirement templates, status dashboard, auto-chasing agents, expiry reminders, audit export.
- **Pricing:** $79 / $179 / $349 a month by active vendors; done-for-you +$200.
- **Competitors:** BCS (free ≤25 vendors; ~$0.95/vendor/mo self-serve), myCOI, TrustLayer, Jones, Billy, PINS, Procore fields.
- **Unfair advantage I could create:** insurance-broker referral channel + a premium-audit report nobody else leads with + hybrid "software + we chase for you" service at SMB prices.
- **Biggest risk:** price compression. The cheap tier is a race to the bottom, so you must win on automation and service, not on storing PDFs.

## 2. Provider credentialing & CAQH re-attestation tracker
- **Why now:** clinician churn and payer scrutiny are high; venture-backed tools have moved upmarket and left small groups behind.
- **Why pay:** one lapsed enrollment = weeks of denied claims ($10k+).
- **Why stay:** 120-day CAQH cycles, constant renewals, and provider history as the source of truth.
- **Find:** CMS NPPES NPI registry (public; filter organizations by taxonomy), state boards, Psychology Today group listings.
- **Contact:** practice-owner Facebook/LinkedIn groups, email to practice managers, billing companies.
- **Sell first:** free "credentialing health check" (exclusion screen + 90-day due list) → $299 setup pilot.
- **MVP:** NPI lookup, credential timeline per provider, CAQH cadence engine, payer enrollment board, reminders, monthly exclusion screen.
- **Pricing:** $35/provider/mo (min $99); firm tier for credentialing freelancers.
- **Competitors:** Medallion, Verifiable, CredentialStream, Modio, symplr, outsourced firms.
- **Unfair advantage:** a multi-client tier for freelance credentialers, who become your sales force.
- **Biggest risk:** SSN/DOB/DEA data. You need a real security program from day one, and practice managers are hard to reach.

## 3. PayrollProof — certified payroll for public-works subcontractors
- **Why now:** the DOL released an updated WH-347 form in January 2025 (contractors had to adapt); there's sustained public infrastructure spending; Gusto has no native certified payroll.
- **Why pay:** without accepted weekly certified payrolls, progress payments get withheld.
- **Why stay:** it's weekly for as long as they work public jobs, which for many subs is permanent.
- **Find:** California DIR contractor registration + PWC-100 project awards (agencies file within 5 days of award), state DOT bid tabs, USAspending subawards.
- **Contact:** phone + email to newly awarded subs ("You just got a public job; certified payroll is due weekly").
- **Sell first:** "We'll produce your next 4 weeks of certified payroll from your existing payroll export," done by hand, for $199.
- **MVP:** payroll CSV import (QuickBooks/Gusto/ADP), worker → classification → rate mapping, wage/fringe checks, WH-347 + Statement of Compliance PDF, one state's format, portal upload file.
- **Pricing:** $99 (1 active project) / $199 (5) / $349 (unlimited).
- **Competitors:** LCPtracker (LCPcertified/Pro), Certified Payroll WH-347 (~$79/mo), eBacon, Points North, construction payroll suites.
- **Unfair advantage:** Gusto-first workflow + pre-submission error checks + a done-for-you option.
- **Biggest risk:** LCPtracker dominance (some agencies pay for it, so contractors use it free) and liability if wage rates are wrong.

## 4. DOT driver qualification files
- **Why now:** new-entrant carriers face a safety audit within 12 months; there's a steady stream of new authorities.
- **Why pay:** audit violations and insurance consequences.
- **Why stay:** med cards, MVRs and reviews renew; records must be kept for employment + 3 years.
- **Find:** FMCSA census (public, many emails/phones) and daily new-authority registers. **The easiest audience of all 40.**
- **Contact:** phone/SMS, bilingual.
- **Sell first:** a free audit-readiness gap report → $199 done-for-you setup.
- **MVP:** 391 checklist per driver, SMS uploads, date extraction, alerts, audit PDF.
- **Pricing:** $49–$149.
- **Competitors:** Core Compliance ($15–$349), Roadworthy HQ ($39–$149), DOTDriverFiles (~$5/user), J.J. Keller, Foley.
- **Unfair advantage:** Spanish-first + done-for-you file building.
- **Biggest risk:** a price war and tiny carriers going out of business.

## 5. CommissionCheck — carrier commission reconciliation for P&C agencies
- **Why now:** AI can now normalize messy PDF/Excel carrier statements, which was impractical before.
- **Why pay:** recovered missing commissions are pure profit, and days of Excel work per month disappear.
- **Why stay:** statements arrive every month forever; carrier mapping rules accumulate.
- **Find:** state DOI agency license databases, Google Maps, Big I / Trusted Choice directories, agency networks.
- **Contact:** email + LinkedIn to agency owners; agency accounting consultants.
- **Sell first:** "Send last month's statements + AMS export; we'll show what you weren't paid," done manually, then a $299 pilot.
- **MVP:** statement upload/forward, AI extraction to a normalized ledger, AMS-export match, exceptions queue, recovery tracker, monthly report.
- **Pricing:** $149 / $299 / $499 by statement volume.
- **Competitors:** Applied Recon (inside Epic), AMS reconciliation features, IVANS commission download, Commission Wizard (from ~$199/mo).
- **Unfair advantage:** focus on non-Epic AMSs and MGA/wholesaler statements that don't arrive via download.
- **Biggest risk:** accuracy across hundreds of statement formats, plus AMS vendors adding this natively.

## 6. Healthcare staffing credential files
- **Why now:** healthcare staffing demand persists; client hospitals audit agencies' files.
- **Why pay:** incomplete files block placements (revenue).
- **Why stay:** every placement and every renewal runs through it.
- **Find:** Google Maps "nurse staffing agency", LinkedIn, state registrations.
- **Contact:** LinkedIn + email to credentialing coordinators.
- **Sell first:** a "file completeness audit" for active travelers/per diem staff.
- **MVP:** candidate doc requests by SMS, expiry engine, client-specific requirement checklists, submission packet PDF.
- **Pricing:** $199–$499.
- **Competitors:** Bullhorn ecosystem, Ceipal, Credentially, ATS modules.
- **Unfair advantage:** client-specific checklist library for regional hospitals.
- **Biggest risk:** the ATS lock-in; this feels like a mid-market sale.

## 7. Veterinary controlled-substance logs
- **Why now:** DEA scrutiny of controlled substances; many independent clinics are still on paper logbooks.
- **Why pay:** DEA fines and registration risk; nightly reconciliation pain.
- **Why stay:** daily logging + records retention.
- **Find:** Google Maps (~30k US clinics), state vet boards.
- **Contact:** practice managers via VHMA groups, phone.
- **Sell first:** "Paper log → digital log setup + audit-ready report."
- **MVP:** drug/bottle inventory, dose logging, running balances, discrepancy alerts, biennial inventory, export.
- **Pricing:** $79–$199.
- **Competitors:** VetSnap, Vet S8, PIMS modules, dispensing cabinets.
- **Unfair advantage:** fastest setup for paper-based independents.
- **Biggest risk:** exact DEA recordkeeping compliance; specialized competitors already exist; clinics tolerate paper.

## 8. 3PL client billing
- **Why now:** e-commerce fulfillment 3PLs multiplied; many run lightweight WMS/shipping stacks with weak billing.
- **Why pay:** unbilled storage/pick fees are lost revenue; billing takes days.
- **Why stay:** monthly invoicing with client rate cards encoded.
- **Find:** Google Maps "3PL fulfillment", fulfillment directories, LinkedIn.
- **Contact:** owners via LinkedIn + email.
- **Sell first:** "We'll rebuild last month's invoices from your exports and show unbilled activity."
- **MVP:** CSV import from WMS/ShipStation, rate-card engine, invoice + detail export, QuickBooks push.
- **Pricing:** $199–$499.
- **Competitors:** WMS billing modules (Extensiv, Logiwa, Packiyo), tariff managers.
- **Unfair advantage:** works with whatever exports they have, no WMS switch.
- **Biggest risk:** integration variety, and WMS vendors improving billing.

## 9. Multi-site facility compliance calendar
- **Why now:** insurers and fire marshals demand inspection proof; facilities teams are thin.
- **Why pay:** fines, insurance issues, life-safety liability.
- **Why stay:** dozens of statutory inspections per site, every year.
- **Find:** harder. LinkedIn facilities directors, private school/diocese directories, IFMA chapters.
- **Contact:** LinkedIn + association events.
- **Sell first:** a "compliance inventory" of one region's sites.
- **MVP:** site × inspection-type matrix, vendor report intake by email, due-date engine, deficiency tracker.
- **Pricing:** $99–$399.
- **Competitors:** CMMS (UpKeep, MaintainX, Limble, Brightly).
- **Unfair advantage:** evidence-first (not work-order-first) design.
- **Biggest risk:** the buyer is hard to list, so sales are slower.

## 10. Food manufacturer supplier documents
- **Why now:** FSMA supplier verification + GFSI audits; small plants priced out of enterprise tools.
- **Why pay:** audit non-conformances can lose retailer accounts.
- **Why stay:** supplier docs expire continuously; audit history.
- **Find:** SQF and BRCGS public certified-site directories.
- **Contact:** QA managers via LinkedIn/email; food safety consultants.
- **Sell first:** a supplier document gap audit before their next SQF audit.
- **MVP:** supplier list, doc requirements per ingredient risk, supplier upload links, expiry engine, audit binder export.
- **Pricing:** $149–$399.
- **Competitors:** TraceGains, Trustwell, SafetyChain.
- **Unfair advantage:** priced and designed for plants under $50M revenue.
- **Biggest risk:** fragmented reach; TraceGains' supplier network effect.
