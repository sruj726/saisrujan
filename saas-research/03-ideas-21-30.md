# Part 1 (cont.) — Ideas 21–30

> Ideas 21–30 are mostly here to show **why** they're traps. Many are the "obvious" ideas from YouTube/Twitter SaaS lists. Rejected ideas are condensed: once a fatal flaw is clear, writing 30 detailed fields would be busywork.

## 21. ShiftProof — janitorial quality inspections & client reporting — **CONDITIONAL / weak**

1. **Name:** ShiftProof
2. **Concept:** Supervisors run phone-based site inspections; clients get automatic monthly quality reports proving the cleaning company did the work.
3. **Customer:** commercial cleaning companies with 10–150 cleaners.
4. **Problem:** client churn from "you're not cleaning properly"; monthly quality reporting.
5. **Current:** paper, Swept, Janitorial Manager, Orange QC, CleanTelligent.
6. **Annoying:** no evidence of quality; manual reports.
7. **Pay monthly:** contracts are monthly, and so are reports.
8. **Price:** $79–$249.
9. **Value:** retaining one $3k/month cleaning contract pays for years.
10. **MVP:** inspection checklists, photos, scoring, auto client reports.
11. **NOT:** scheduling, payroll, time clock.
12–15. 4/10 difficulty; AI unnecessary.
16. **Competitors:** Swept, Orange QC, Janitorial Manager, CleanTelligent.
17–18. Decent tools already exist at fair prices.
19–22. Janitorial associations (ISSA, BSCAI), Facebook groups.
23. **Churn:** medium-high (thin-margin owners).
24–30. **Verdict:** real pain, but cheap, competent incumbents and price-sensitive buyers.

## 22. RankRecap — SEO/PPC client reporting for agencies — **REJECT**

- **Concept/customer:** automated white-label reports for marketing agencies.
- **Why it fails:** AgencyAnalytics, DashThis, Whatagraph, Swydo, and Looker Studio (free) own this. AI-written commentary is now a checkbox feature. Agencies churn constantly.
- **Price/churn:** $49–$299; 5–8%/mo.
- **Verdict:** the most saturated "boring SaaS" category there is.

## 23. SiteCare Reports — WordPress maintenance reports for web agencies — **REJECT**

- **Concept:** updates, backups, uptime, and branded monthly reports.
- **Why it fails:** ManageWP (free tier), MainWP (open source), WP Umbrella, GoDaddy Pro. Commoditized; per-site prices pushed to $1–$3.
- **Verdict:** no room for pricing power.

## 24. MissedCall Rescue — missed-call text-back for home services — **REJECT**

- **Concept:** auto-text callers you missed, capture leads.
- **Why it fails:** every GoHighLevel reseller sells this for $97–$297 bundled with a CRM. Field-service platforms (Housecall Pro, Jobber, ServiceTitan) and phone systems include it. Also A2P 10DLC registration friction.
- **Verdict:** a feature that has been commoditized by agencies.

## 25. ReviewLoop — review requests & AI responses for local businesses — **REJECT**

- **Why it fails:** Birdeye, Podium, NiceJob, Grade.us, GatherUp, plus GHL resellers and native Google features. High churn because owners can't see the ROI after month 3.
- **Verdict:** a commodity with high acquisition cost.

## 26. OnboardKit — new-hire paperwork for small businesses — **REJECT**

- **Why it fails:** Gusto, Rippling, BambooHR, Justworks, and every payroll provider bundle onboarding free. Low frequency for a 20-person company (a few hires per year).
- **Verdict:** bundled away by payroll.

## 27. W9Collect — vendor W-9 collection & 1099 prep — **REJECT as standalone**

- **Why it fails:** the pain peaks in January, so it's seasonal and churns after filing season. Track1099/Tax1099/QuickBooks/Gusto/Bill.com cover it. TIN-matching is useful but commoditized.
- **Salvage:** keep it as a **feature inside SubShield** (vendors already upload COIs, so also collect the W-9).

## 28. CloseClock — real estate transaction coordinator deadline tracker — **REJECT**

- **Why it fails:** Dotloop, SkySlope, Paperless Pipeline, Open to Close, ListedKit, plus brokerage-provided tools. Agents are a high-churn, deal-flow-dependent market; 2023–2025 transaction volume slumps showed how fragile it is.
- **Verdict:** crowded and cyclical.

## 29. GrantGuard — grant reporting deadline & deliverable tracker for nonprofits — **REJECT**

- **Why it fails:** low willingness to pay (the money comes from overhead budgets nonprofits are pressured to minimize); Instrumentl, Submittable, Fluxx, Foundant; long board-approval cycles.
- **Verdict:** real pain, weak wallet.

## 30. RatioReady — childcare center staff file & licensing compliance — **CONDITIONAL / weak**

1. **Name:** RatioReady
2. **Concept:** Tracks each childcare staffer's background check, CPR/first aid, TB test, and annual training hours against state licensing rules, plus center license renewals and inspection prep.
3. **Customer:** independent childcare centers and small chains (1–10 centers).
4. **Problem:** state inspectors review staff files; missing training hours or expired checks lead to citations.
5. **Current:** paper binders, Brightwheel/Procare (staff features vary), spreadsheets.
6. **Annoying:** high staff turnover makes the files constantly incomplete.
7. **Pay monthly:** turnover plus annual hour requirements.
8. **Price:** $49–$149/center.
9. **Value:** avoided citations; inspection readiness.
10. **MVP:** staff roster, state rule template (1 state), doc upload, hour tracking, alerts, inspector-ready export.
11. **NOT:** parent communication, billing, attendance (Brightwheel owns these).
12–15. 4/10 difficulty; AI unnecessary.
16. **Competitors:** Brightwheel, Procare, Kangarootime, Playground, plus state-run training registries.
17. **Gaps:** staff compliance is often an afterthought in the parent-facing platforms.
18. **Differentiate:** state-specific rules.
19–22. State childcare associations; licensing consultants.
23. **Churn:** medium (thin-margin centers).
24. **Cancel:** the all-in-one platform adds the feature.
25–30. Low cost and high margin, but buyers on thin margins mean low WTP. Background check data is sensitive. **Verdict:** possible, but the platforms are likely to bundle it and budgets are tight.
