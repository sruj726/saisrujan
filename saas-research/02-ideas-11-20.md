11. **NOT:** negotiation services, spend management.

13. **Integrations:** Google Drive, email, Ramp/Brex later.
14. **AI?** Yes, for extraction.

16. **Competitors:** Vendr, Zylo, Spendflo, Cledara, Tropic, ContractSafe, Ramp/Brex built-ins.

18. **Differentiate:** hard.
19–22. Content/SEO only. No obvious channel.
23. **Churn:** high (set up once, then forgotten).
24. **Cancel:** "it only saved us once."
25–26. Spend analytics, negotiation service.

## 12. EntityDesk — annual report & entity compliance tracker for CPA/law firms — **CONDITIONAL**

1. **Name:** EntityDesk
2. **Concept:** Tracks state annual reports, franchise taxes, registered-agent renewals, and foreign qualifications for every client entity a CPA or law firm manages.
3. **Customer:** small CPA firms and business-law firms managing 50–2,000 client entities.
4. **Problem:** each state has different due dates and fees; missed filings cause penalties and administrative dissolution.
5. **Current:** practice-management custom fields, spreadsheets, registered-agent reminder emails, enterprise tools (CSC, CT Corporation, Diligent).
6. **Annoying:** 50 state rule sets; clients ignore reminders; liability for the firm.
7. **Pay monthly:** a rolling calendar of deadlines across clients.
8. **Price:** $79–$299 (by entity count).
9. **Value:** avoided penalties and reinstatement fees; it also creates billable work for the firm.
10. **MVP:** entity list import, state rules engine (start with 10 states), deadline calendar, client reminder emails, status tracking.
11. **NOT:** filing on the client's behalf, registered-agent service, all 50 states.
12. **Workflow:** import entities → deadlines generated → firm + client reminders → mark filed → archive confirmation.
13. **Integrations:** Secretary of State business search (mostly scraping/manual, varies by state), email, Karbon/Canopy later.
14. **AI?** Not necessary.
15. **Difficulty:** 4/10 tech; state-rule maintenance is ongoing.
16. **Competitors:** CSC, CT Corporation, Diligent Entities, Harbor Compliance, registered-agent firms' free reminders.
17. **Gaps:** enterprise tools are overkill for a 10-person CPA firm.
18. **Differentiate:** firm-centric multi-client UX; upsell filing-as-a-service work to the firm's clients.
19. **Acquisition:** CPA communities, state CPA societies.
20. **Niche:** CPA firms with multistate small-business clients.
21–22. CPA society sponsorships + practice-management marketplaces.
23. **Churn:** low (2–3%).
24. **Cancel:** registered-agent firms bundle reminders free.
25. **Retention:** filing history archive.
26. **Upsells:** filing service via a partner.
27–28. Low cost, 85% margin.
29. Low sensitivity.
30. Entity management for SMBs. **Cautionary tale:** the 2024 boom in BOI-reporting startups collapsed in March 2025 when FinCEN exempted US companies. **Never build on a single new regulation that politics can reverse.**

## 13. LienClock — preliminary notice & lien deadline tracker for subcontractors — **CONDITIONAL**

1. **Name:** LienClock
2. **Concept:** Calculates state-specific preliminary notice and mechanic's lien deadlines for every job and reminds you to send notices before your payment rights expire.
3. **Customer:** subcontractors and suppliers ($2–50M) working in notice-heavy states (CA, TX, FL, AZ).
4. **Problem:** missing a notice deadline forfeits lien rights, which is the main leverage for getting paid.
5. **Current:** Levelset (now Procore Pay), attorneys, spreadsheets, Handle, Siteline (pay apps).
6. **Annoying:** complex state rules; per-job tracking.
7. **Pay monthly:** new jobs every month.
8. **Price:** $99–$299.
9. **Value:** protects receivables worth 10–100× the fee.
10. **MVP:** job entry, state deadline calculator (3 states), reminders, notice templates.
11. **NOT:** mailing notices (certified mail), lien filing service.
12. **Workflow:** add job → deadlines → reminders → mark sent.
13. **Integrations:** certified-mail API later, QBO.
14. **AI?** No.
15. **Difficulty:** 5/10 (the legal rules must be right).
16. **Competitors:** Levelset/Procore (dominant), Handle, legal services.
17. **Gaps:** Levelset is strong; its free calculators eat the low end.
18. **Differentiate:** bundled with COI/pay-app workflows.
19–22. Supplier/subcontractor associations.
23. **Churn:** medium.
24. **Cancel:** "We only use it when we're not paid."
25–26. Notice-mailing service and filing service upsells.
27–28. Low cost; mailing is pass-through.
29. **High legal risk** (wrong deadlines = lost lien rights; arguably practicing law in some framings).
30. Construction payments. **Verdict:** a dominant incumbent and legal-liability exposure.

## 16. EligiBot — dental insurance eligibility & benefits verification — **CONDITIONAL** (high pain, hard build)

1. **Name:** EligiBot
2. **Concept:** Automatically verifies every scheduled patient's dental insurance eligibility and benefits 2–3 days before the appointment and writes a clean breakdown back to the practice.
3. **Customer:** dental practices with 1–10 locations.
4. **Problem:** front-desk staff spend hours a day on payer portals and phone calls verifying coverage.
5. **Current:** manual portal checks/phone calls, outsourced offshore verification teams ($300–$1,000+/mo), Vyne Trellis, Zuub, Onederful, DentalXChange, AI voice-agent startups.
6. **Annoying:** time-consuming, error-prone; surprise patient balances.
7. **Pay monthly:** every patient visit.
8. **Price:** $299–$799/location.
9. **Value:** 1–2 FTE of front-desk time ($3k–$8k/month).
10. **MVP:** schedule import, eligibility via clearinghouse API (270/271) + payer portal fallback done manually, benefits summary PDF.
11. **NOT:** voice AI calling, claims, PMS write-back for all PMS systems.
12. **Workflow:** pull schedule → verify → summary attached → exceptions flagged.
13. **Integrations:** clearinghouse eligibility APIs, Open Dental API (relatively open), Dentrix/Eaglesoft (harder).
14. **AI?** Yes, for messy benefit breakdowns (and voice agents in time).
15. **Difficulty:** 8/10 (PMS integrations, payer variance, PHI).
16. **Competitors:** Vyne, Zuub, Onederful, DentalXChange, several AI startups, offshore services.
17. **Gaps:** dental 271 data is often incomplete; a "full breakdown" still needs humans.
18. **Differentiate:** hybrid AI + human service with SLA.
19–22. Dental consultants, DSOs, study clubs.
23. **Churn:** low if accurate; high after errors.
24. **Cancel:** accuracy problems, PMS changes.
25–26. Claims follow-up, patient estimates.
27. API fees + human verification labor.
28. Margin 50–70% (service-heavy).
29. **HIPAA: PHI throughout — BAA, security program, audit logs. Mandatory.**
30. RCM for dental. **Verdict:** a great market for a team with dental/RCM experience; too heavy and competitive for a 2–6 week solo MVP.
